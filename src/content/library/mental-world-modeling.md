synopsis: "This paper introduces Mental World Modeling, a framework integrating physical and mental state variables to predict human actions. The authors propose MENTIS, a training-free baseline that parses scenes, generates target-specific observations, and simulates coupled transitions. Experiments across eight large language models demonstrate that explicitly tracking mental states is essential for accurate decision prediction, highlighting current bottlenecks in existing world modeling approaches."
takeaways:

  - "Candidate actions are decomposed to update both physical and mental components."
  - "The MENTIS baseline is training-free and fully inspectable across all stages."
